How to look

Wiggle
Nothing needed. The two photos swap quickly; your brain reads the shifting as depth.
Red/Cyan
Put on red-left, cyan-right paper glasses.
Headset
Use a Google Cardboard-style viewer or VR headset with your phone sideways, in full screen.
Cross-eye
Look at the pair and cross your eyes until three images appear; focus on the middle one. Depth may need a moment to settle.

Use the arrow keys or space bar to advance, and the Depth focus buttons if the scene looks strained.

Photographs: Stereograph Cards, Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division. The Library lists these cards as having no known restrictions on publication. Each card was cropped from its scan, its two halves aligned for height and convergence, and (when printed in reverse order) swapped. Part of Yesterdial, which also streams from the Internet Archive.
